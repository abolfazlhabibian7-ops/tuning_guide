Where Do I Start?

  1. Define your goal: daily comfort, speed or looks.
  2. Set a realistic budget, including installation.
  3. Check local laws, insurance and warranty.
  4. Choose a trusted, experienced workshop.

FAQ

Does tuning void my warranty?

It can. Always check your warranty terms before modifying the car.

Which upgrade should I do first?

Most people start with tires and suspension, because they improve safety and feel.
